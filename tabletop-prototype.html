The Bell Beneath the Mire

The party reaches a half-sunken chapel at the edge of the Mire. Beneath the water, a bell rings once every few minutes. Each ring causes someone in the nearby village to forget a loved one's name.

Immediate danger

The chapel floor is collapsing. A trapped villager is alive in the bell chamber, but the only safe path runs through a flooded crypt marked with the party's own names.
